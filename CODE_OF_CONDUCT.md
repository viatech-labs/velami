# Code of conduct

We want Velami to be a project where anyone can take part without harassment, whatever their
experience, background or identity.

Be respectful and assume good intent. Criticize code and ideas, not people. No harassment, insults,
sexual language or imagery, and no publishing anyone's private information. This applies to
issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and contributions that break these rules, and may ban
people who keep breaking them.

Report problems to info@via-tech.org. Reports are handled confidentially.

Based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
